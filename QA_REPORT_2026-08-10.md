## 3. Bug found & fixed during QA

**Skill selector crash** (`computer-skill-library.ts`): `selectComputerSkills` passed `skill.trigger` (a `string[]`) to `tokenize()` which expects a `string`. This caused `TypeError: (s || "").toLowerCase is not a function`, making `lc-agent-plan` silently fall back to a generic 5-block plan.

- **Fix:** `tokenize(skill.trigger.join(" "))`.
- **Verified:** after re-deploy, returns a real 15-block plan via the live model (no fallback).

The fallback safety net worked as designed — it returned a valid (if generic) plan with `is_fallback:true` rather than erroring, which is why initial QA caught this only as degraded output, not a crash.
